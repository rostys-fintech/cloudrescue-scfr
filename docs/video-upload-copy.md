## Description

Resilience Atlas is an interactive synthetic simulator for systemic cloud-resilience risk in banking.

It explores what happens when multiple synthetic banks depend on the same shared cloud provider and need recovery capacity at the same time.

The prototype compares three recovery mechanisms:

- Post-shock Market Sourcing
- Individual Reserves
- SCFR Pooled Reserve

The central controlled comparison is:

**Same shock. Same reserve budget. Different coordination.**

The demo also shows the Scenario Lab, a local 3×3 Robustness Sweep, the real-vs-synthetic evidence boundary, and the target-user framing for bank resilience teams, supervisors and researchers.

Important boundary: the network, banks, provider assignments and all numerical outputs are synthetic and illustrative. Resilience Atlas is a mechanism stress-test, not a forecast or an assessment of real institutions.
