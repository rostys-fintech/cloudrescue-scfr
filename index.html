RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

Transparent assumptions. Reproducible scenarios. Explicit guardrails.

CloudRescue separates the observed concentration-risk problem from the synthetic mechanism used to explore it.

MODEL LOGIC

Transparent by design

  1. One synthetic provider fails.
  2. All banks connected to it simultaneously require backup capacity.
  3. Spot-market capacity is scarce.
  4. Individual reserves are ring-fenced bank by bank.
  5. SCFR receives the same reserve budget, but pools it across affected banks.
  6. Recovery is limited by both capacity and each bank's failover readiness.
HEADLINE METRIC

Systemic Resilience Score

SRS = Σ(wᵢ × rᵢ) / Σ(wᵢ) × 100

wᵢ combines critical workload and stylized systemic importance. rᵢ is the restored fraction after capacity allocation and readiness constraints.

v0.1 is intentionally synthetic. Empirical calibration is a future research stage.

REAL-WORLD MOTIVATION

The problem is observed. The numbers are synthetic.

CloudRescue does not assume that 20 named banks literally use three named providers. The prototype uses a stylized network to stress-test a real concentration-risk mechanism documented by BIS, DORA and the EBA.
